10. TEST PERSON_ID + PAPER_ID + ALERT BANG WEBCAM
------------------------------------------------

Lenh:

    python -m backend.ai_services.object_detect.test_paper_tracking_webcam --source 0

Lenh nay tu dong bat che do webcam toi uu:

    - Ha frame Full HD ve toi da 960x540 va giu dung ti le.
    - Camera buffer = 1 de khong doc cac frame cu bi ton.
    - Person YOLO chay moi 2 frame, frame giua dung detection cache.
    - Object hybrid chay moi 4 frame.
    - best (1).pt phat hien object chuyen biet cua bai toan.
    - yolov8n.pt pretrained phat hien person, cell phone, book va remote.
    - Confidence smartphone tren webcam co san toi thieu 0.50.
    - Neu pretrained thay remote trung voi phone, ung vien phone bi loai.
    - Neu crop co luoi nut bam vat ly it nhat 3 hang x 3 cot, no duoc xem
      la calculator va khong tao smartphone alert.

Lan inference dau tien co the dung 1-2 giay de warm-up model. Tu frame sau,
thoi gian object inference CPU thuong con khoang 0.16-0.30 giay va chi chay
moi 4 frame.

Quy trinh test dung:

    1. Dua nguoi vao camera.
    2. Khi hien ASSIGN PERSON, go ID ngay trong cua so camera.
       Vi du: STUDENT_01, sau do nhan ENTER.
    3. Dua to de thi hop le vao camera.
    4. Khi hien ASSIGN PAPER, nhap paper ID dang so duong.
       Vi du: 101, sau do nhan ENTER.
    5. Cho den khi person va paper deu hien memory=on.
    6. Nhan A de ARM/khoa dang ky.
    7. Dua to de cu ra ngoai roi dua lai:
       he thong phai khoi phuc dung paper_id=101 va khong alert.
    8. Dua mot to giay/quyen tap khac vao:
       he thong phai tao paper_id khac va alert cho dung thi sinh.

Phim dieu khien:

    - ENTER: luu ID dang nhap.
    - BACKSPACE: xoa ky tu.
    - ESC khi dang nhap: bo qua temporary ID hien tai.
    - A: ARM, khoa dang ky de thi.
    - D: mo lai che do dang ky.
    - Q hoac ESC khi khong dang nhap: thoat.

Khong the ARM neu:

    - Con person/paper chua duoc gan ID.
    - Person fingerprint chua tao duoc.
    - Paper fingerprint chua tao duoc.

Khi do can de mat, than nguoi va to giay ro hon, du sang hon.
